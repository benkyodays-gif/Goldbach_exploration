# Observations

I ran the program up to n = 20,000 and plotted the number of 
prime-pair representations for each even number.

The first thing I noticed was how structured the distribution looked.
I expected individual points, but the overall pattern was much more 
visible than I expected.

Some even numbers have many more prime representations than others.
The number of representations also seems to increase overall as n becomes larger,
although the distribution is not smooth.

This is only a computational exploration. It does not prove Goldbach's conjecture.

## Questions I want to explore

- Why does the distribution have this shape?
- Why do some even numbers have many more prime representations?
- How does the pattern change when the upper limit is increased?
- What role does the distribution of primes play in the pattern?
